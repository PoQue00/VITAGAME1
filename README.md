HOW TO MAKE YOUR OWN LEVELS:


STEP 1: INSTALL GODOT 3.5 FROM https://github.com/SonicMastr/godot-vita/releases
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="chrome_pwV4H7a6oA" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1a344661-baf1-4f63-a351-2c97f620a7e1" />


STEP 2: OPEN THE ENGINE

GO TO YOUR DOWNLOADS FOLDER AND DOUBLE CLICK THE .EXE FILE
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="03zhjoXAx5" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/13914f91-5e75-42c9-8816-9639149a9979" />


STEP 3: DOWNLOADING THE PROJECT

ON THE TOP OF THIS PAGE PRESS CODE AND DOWNLOAD ZIP
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="chrome_iQQgxlzVSP" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6a36a56a-a299-4472-af5f-49801d6645ee" />

GO TO YOUR DOWNLOADS FOLDER
RIGHT CLICK THE ZIP FILE AND PRESS EXTRACT
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="DnijAYYGUH" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9b6565c7-e1d0-4d38-b444-670010c6a5f5" />


STEP 4: IMPORTING TO GODOT

GO BACK TO GODOT
OPEN YOUR FILE BROWSER AND FIND "project.godot" AND CLICK IT ONCE
THEN DRAG AND DROP THE FILE INTO THE GODOT WINDOW
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="BCGQLnQxoX" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/89ca66f0-04ad-4eb0-b812-6312f9464bb9" />


STEP 5: OPENING THE PROJECT

IN GODOT DOUBLE CLICK THE "PLATFORMER PROJECT (PC)"
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Godot_v3 5-rc5-vita_4dMo5GlUxO" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ca325571-4099-4520-8d42-be6a4a25576e" />


STEP 6: CREATING A LEVEL

IN GODOT RIGHT CLICK ON THE "Scene" FOLDER AND PRESS CREATE SCENE
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Godot_v3 5-rc5-vita_PtBSKfpYDi" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c59c911b-b9ab-4b74-8bc3-48bb8731fc1a" />

NAME IT WHATEVER YOU WANT AND PRESS OK
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Godot_v3 5-rc5-vita_VSLuy2gg7H" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9927bf07-d561-4499-8aef-1d0ce39a9576" />


STEP 7: EDITING THE LEVEL

IN YOUR NEW SCENE PRESS NEW 2D NODE
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Godot_v3 5-rc5-vita_679wKwLPKT" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a57fdd3b-29ca-4641-8265-f9b4b9771abf" />

DOUBLE CLICK IT TO RENAME IT TO YOUR LEVEL NAME

PRESS ARROW NEXT TO SCENE TO OPEN THE SCENE FOLDER
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Godot_v3 5-rc5-vita_fmOypwealV" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/994f3a62-089a-43bc-9ab3-ab856cde222f" />

SCROLL UNTIL YOU FIND TILEMAP
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Godot_v3 5-rc5-vita_HdtJglUHAv" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/25aad3fc-38e1-4ee3-ba3d-a1c227f39617" />

DRAG AND DROP TILEMAP ONTO YOUR NODE2D
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Godot_v3 5-rc5-vita_wl59VxiQWl" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/313086bf-d836-4b3c-8b86-7309a35341c4" />

SELECT THE TILEMAP UNDER YOUR NODE AND SELCECT THE "testtileset.png 0"
<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="Godot_v3 5-rc5-vita_YYWm97FNLb" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/06698a96-8ad6-4f94-8ec5-36146cdbc179" />
